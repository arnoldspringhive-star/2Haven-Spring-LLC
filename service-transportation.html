Transportation Coordination

Safe, reliable, and comfortable transportation arrangements for essential appointments and leisurely outings.

Reliable transportation is essential for maintaining health and independence. We coordinate safe, comfortable travel arrangements for everything from critical wellness appointments to leisurely scenic drives.

Our team aims to support that residents arrive at their destinations safely, on time, and without the stress of navigating transit systems or traffic.


What's Included

  • wellness appointments: Arranging rides and providing accompaniment to doctors or therapy.
  • Errand Assistance: Transportation for grocery shopping, banking, or personal needs.
  • Leisure Outings: Coordinated travel for family visits or recreational activities.
  • Accessibility: Ensuring all transportation methods accommodate the physical needs of the resident.

Reliable Mobility & Travel Coordination

Maintaining access to medical clinics, community destinations, and family gatherings is vital for a fulfilling life. HavenSpring LLC provides coordinated, accessible transportation support throughout Ashwaubenon, Green Bay, De Pere, and greater Brown County. We organize scheduled travel for physician visits, specialist consultations, banking, personal shopping, and recreational outings.

All transportation arrangements are selected to accommodate resident mobility aids, wheelchairs, walkers, and personal comfort requirements.

Stress-Free Transit & Caregiver Accompaniment

Travel can sometimes cause anxiety for adults requiring residential care. Our staff coordinates trip timing, assists with boarding and vehicle transfers, and provides accompaniment when required so residents feel safe and at ease throughout their journey.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should transportation be scheduled?

We recommend arranging non-emergency medical transportation at least 48 to 72 hours in advance to guarantee vehicle scheduling.

Are vehicles wheelchair and walker accessible?

Yes, all coordinated travel services utilize accessibility-equipped vehicles tailored to each resident's physical mobility needs.

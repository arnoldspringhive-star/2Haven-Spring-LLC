Service Details

Health and Appointment Coordination

Proactive management of healthcare schedules, facilitating seamless communication with health care professionals.

Navigating the healthcare system can be overwhelming. We act as a proactive bridge between residents, families, and health care professionals, ensuring that health needs are seamlessly managed.

From scheduling check-ups to communicating with specialists, we keep everyone informed and aim to verify that wellness advice is properly integrated into the daily care plan.


What's Included

  • Scheduling: Booking and managing dates for doctors, dentists, and specialists.
  • Communication: Acting as a liaison to relay information between doctors and family members.
  • Follow-up Care: Ensuring post-appointment instructions are clearly understood and followed.
  • Health Tracking: Monitoring symptoms or changes in condition to report to health teams.

Seamless Healthcare & Clinical Coordination

Navigating doctors' visits, specialist referrals, physical therapy, and routine screenings can be overwhelming for families. HavenSpring LLC serves as a reliable healthcare coordination hub in Ashwaubenon and Green Bay, WI. We organize appointment schedules, arrange transportation, compile vital health records, and facilitate clear communication between visiting healthcare practitioners, primary physicians, and resident families.

We work collaboratively with local healthcare networks including Bellin Health, Aurora BayCare Medical Center, HSHS St. Vincent, and Prevea Health to ensure consistent medical continuity.

Care Continuity & Transparent Family Updates

Following every doctor appointment or clinical consultation, our staff logs instructions, prescription adjustments, and therapy recommendations into the resident's individualized care file, keeping family members promptly informed of any medical updates.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do staff accompany residents to outside medical visits?

Yes, staff coordination and accompaniment are available to ensure residents arrive safely and all physician instructions are accurately documented.

Can visiting therapists or home health nurses see residents at HavenSpring?

Absolutely. We warmly accommodate visiting physical therapists, occupational therapists, and home health nurses in our residence.

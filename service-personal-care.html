Service Details

Personal Care Assistance

Dignified and respectful support with daily living activities, intended to preserve independence and comfort.

We believe that personal care should be delivered with the utmost dignity and respect. Our compassionate team provides sensitive, hands-on assistance with Activities of Daily Living (ADLs) to help residents maintain their hygiene and confidence.

By focusing on the individual's comfort level and preferences, we aim to support that personal care routines are a positive, stress-free part of the day.


What's Included

  • Bathing & Grooming: Respectful assistance with showering, oral care, and personal hygiene.
  • Dressing Assistance: Help selecting outfits and getting dressed comfortably each day.
  • Mobility Support: Safe assistance with transfers, walking, and positioning.
  • Incontinence Care: Discreet and prompt support to support cleanliness and comfort.

Dignity-Centered Care Protocols

At HavenSpring LLC, we recognize that receiving assistance with personal care requires trust, compassion, and sensitivity. Our caregivers are thoroughly trained in person-centered care techniques that promote independence wherever possible while offering safe, hands-on assistance when needed. Every resident's daily routine is carefully structured according to their individual comfort, personal preferences, and schedule.

Our personal care protocols adhere strictly to Wisconsin residential care guidelines, ensuring discreet hygiene routines, skin integrity monitoring, and adaptive equipment assistance in our fully accessible Ashwaubenon residence.

Why Families in Brown County Trust HavenSpring

Families throughout Ashwaubenon, Green Bay, and the surrounding Brown County area choose HavenSpring because of our continuous commitment to empathetic, dignified caregiving. We work closely with family members, case managers, and healthcare providers to ensure that personal care plans evolve seamlessly as individual needs change.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you respect a resident's privacy during personal care?

All personal care assistance is conducted one-on-one behind closed doors with gentle, professional caregivers who prioritize resident comfort, modesty, and emotional well-being at every step.

Can care routines be customized to morning or evening preferences?

Yes. We tailor all waking, bathing, and dressing schedules to match the resident's natural daily rhythm and personal lifestyle preferences.

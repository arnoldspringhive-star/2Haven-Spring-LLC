Service Details

Companionship and Social Engagement

A vibrant community atmosphere fostering joyful interactions, engaging activities, and meaningful relationships.

Mental and emotional well-being are just as important as physical health. Our companionship services are designed to combat loneliness and foster a vibrant, joyful community atmosphere.

Our caregivers take the time to build genuine relationships, engaging in meaningful conversations, hobbies, and activities that spark joy and cognitive stimulation.


What's Included

  • Engaging Activities: Playing games, reading, doing puzzles, or enjoying music.
  • Meaningful Conversation: Active listening and sharing stories to build strong bonds.
  • Hobby Support: Assisting residents in pursuing their personal interests and passions.
  • Emotional Support: Providing a trusted, friendly confidant to share daily experiences with.

Enriching Social Lives & Emotional Wellness

Social isolation can significantly impact emotional and physical health. At HavenSpring LLC, companionship is woven into the fabric of daily life. Our caregivers take time to listen to stories, participate in favorite pastimes, play board games, engage in arts and crafts, and celebrate holidays and personal milestones together.

We nurture a family-like atmosphere where every resident feels valued, heard, and connected to a compassionate community in Ashwaubenon, WI.

Tailored Activities for Every Personality

Whether a resident thrives in group activities or prefers quiet, one-on-one conversations with a book or cup of tea, our activities program respects individual social comfort levels while encouraging meaningful daily engagement.

Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of daily activities are organized?

Activities include music therapy, memory games, gardening, reading, seasonal crafts, movie afternoons, and birthday celebrations.

Are family visits encouraged?

Yes! We maintain an open, welcoming environment for family and friends to visit and spend quality time with their loved ones.

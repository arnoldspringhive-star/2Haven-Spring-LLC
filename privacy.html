Privacy Policy

Your Privacy is Important to Us

At HavenSpring LLC, we are committed to protecting the privacy and security of our residents, their families, and our website visitors. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Information We Collect

We only collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when using our contact or career forms, such as your name, phone number, email address, and reason for inquiry. Do not submit sensitive health or personal data through these forms.

How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used solely to respond to your inquiries, process employment applications, and provide you with information regarding our adult residential care services.

Data Security

We take appropriate security measures to protect against unauthorized access or disclosure of the information we collect. However, no internet transmission is entirely secure.

Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at havenspringllc@gmail.com.

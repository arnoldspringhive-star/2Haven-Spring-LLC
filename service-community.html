Community Integration

Supported outings and activities that encourage active participation in the broader local community.

Staying connected to the wider world is vital for a fulfilling life. We actively support residents in maintaining their ties to the local community through planned outings and participation in local events.

Whether it's a trip to a local park, attending a community gathering, or shopping, We aim to offer the support needed to safely navigate the world outside our doors.


What's Included

  • Supervised Outings: Safe, accompanied trips to local parks, stores, and community centers.
  • Event Participation: Support in attending local cultural, religious, or social events.
  • Skill Building: Helping residents practice independence in public settings.
  • Social Networking: Facilitating opportunities to meet and interact with the broader community.

Active Brown County Community Participation

At HavenSpring LLC, we believe that adult residential care should be integrated with the vibrant life of our surrounding community. Located in Ashwaubenon near Green Bay, WI, we organize supported outings to local parks, community centers, botanical gardens, shopping districts, and cultural venues. Community involvement enhances cognitive engagement, boosts mood, and reinforces a strong sense of belonging.

Our team plans every outing with accessibility in mind, ensuring manageable walking distances, restful seating availability, and continuous staff supervision.

Empowering Individual Interests

We work with residents to identify personal interests—whether that means visiting a local library, enjoying an ice cream outing, or attending local music and community events—helping each individual stay connected to the places they love.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are community outings supervised by staff?

Yes, dedicated caregivers accompany residents on all group and individual community outings to ensure safety and comfort.

Are outings mandatory or optional?

All outings are entirely optional. Residents choose activities based on their personal desires, energy levels, and interests.

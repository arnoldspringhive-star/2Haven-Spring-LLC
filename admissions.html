Admissions & Referrals

Our Planned Admissions Process

We are developing a thoughtful, step-by-step process intended to help ensure that HavenSpring can appropriately support the unique needs of each individual we aim to serve.

1. Initial Inquiry

The process is intended to begin with an initial conversation. Families or referring partners may reach out to discuss an individual's general situation, helping determine whether an assessment may be an appropriate next step.

2. Comprehensive Assessment

Prior to any decisions, a thorough assessment may be considered to better understand care needs, mobility considerations, and personal preferences, as well as overall compatibility with the environment being developed.

3. Review & Transition Planning

Any potential placement would be based on assessment outcomes and overall compatibility. If alignment is identified, a transition approach may be outlined to support a smooth and respectful move.

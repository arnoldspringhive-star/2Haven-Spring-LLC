About Us

About HavenSpring LLC

HavenSpring LLC is a locally owned adult residential care organization located in the Village of Ashwaubenon in Brown County, Wisconsin.

Our Philosophy

Our Approach

HavenSpring was established to provide adults age 18 and older with individualized care and support in a small, welcoming, and homelike setting. Our approach is based on dignity, respect, safety, personal choice, cultural awareness, and community inclusion.

Who We Serve

  • • Frail elderly individuals
  • • Individuals with dementia or cognitive impairments
  • • Individuals with physical disabilities
  • • Individuals with intellectual or developmental disabilities
  • • Individuals with traumatic brain injuries
  • • Individuals with stable mental health conditions

Admission & Care

Every prospective resident is evaluated individually to determine if safe placement is possible. Admission depends on authorized capacity, home accessibility, and our ability to safely and appropriately meet the person's unique needs.

Our Process

Developing a Journey of Care

From the initial consultation through daily routines, HavenSpring is being thoughtfully developed with a structured and compassionate approach in mind. The goal is to support comfort and overall well-being at each stage.

01

Consultation

The process is intended to begin with a thoughtful conversation to better understand individual needs, preferences, and daily routines before exploring appropriate next steps.

02

Care Planning

If an assessment suggests compatibility, a plan may be outlined to reflect individual support preferences, daily structure, and engagement considerations.

03

Matching

The approach is intended to consider compatibility between individuals and caregivers based on skills, communication styles, and shared preferences.

04

Ongoing Support

Plans may be reviewed periodically to reflect changes in needs, preferences, and overall well-being over time.

Respite Care

Temporary, compassionate residential support providing a restful break for primary caregivers and families.

Caregiving is a labor of love, but everyone needs a break to recharge. Our respite care services offer temporary, compassionate residential support so primary caregivers can take time for themselves with total peace of mind.

During their stay, respite residents receive the same high-quality, individualized care, delicious meals, and engaging activities as our long-term residents.


What's Included

  • Seamless Transitions: A smooth onboarding process to make short-term stays comfortable and familiar.
  • Full Support: Access to all 24-hour supervision, personal care, and meal services.
  • Caregiver Relief: A planned safe environment that allows family members to truly rest.
  • Flexible Stays: Accommodating short-term care needs, whether for a few days or a few weeks.

Supportive Relief for Family Caregivers

Caring for an adult family member is deeply rewarding, but it can also be physically and emotionally demanding. HavenSpring LLC offers short-term residential respite care in Ashwaubenon, WI, providing primary caregivers with the opportunity to travel, attend to personal obligations, or simply rest and recharge with complete peace of mind.

Respite residents receive the exact same high-standard 24/7 care, nutritious home-cooked meals, medication support, and engaging companionship as our full-time residents.

A Smooth, Welcoming Respite Stay

We work closely with families prior to arrival to review medical routines, favorite foods, and personal preferences, ensuring that temporary guests transition smoothly into a safe, friendly home environment.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum length of stay for respite care?

Respite care stays can range from several days to several weeks, depending on room availability and family scheduling needs.

Can a respite stay transition into long-term residential placement?

Yes, a respite stay is often an ideal way for families and prospective residents to experience our care community before making a permanent placement decision.

Service Details

24-Hour Support and Supervision

Peace of mind with around-the-clock staffing, aiming to provide immediate assistance and constant supervision day or night.

Peace of mind is paramount when it comes to the safety of your loved ones. Our 24-hour support aims to support that residents are never alone and always have access to immediate assistance whenever they need it, day or night.

Our awake-staff model provides continuous supervision, reducing the risk of falls, wandering, or emergency situations while maintaining a comforting, watchful presence.


What's Included

  • Continuous Monitoring: Awake, alert staff available 24/7.
  • Emergency Response: Immediate action and support during any unexpected health or safety events.
  • Nighttime Assistance: Help with restroom visits, mobility, and comfort during the night.
  • Reassurance: A comforting presence that reduces anxiety for both residents and their families.

Continuous 24/7 Awake Care & Immediate Response

Around-the-clock peace of mind is the cornerstone of HavenSpring LLC. Our adult residential facility maintains awake, attentive staff 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year. Whether a resident requires mobility assistance in the middle of the night, emotional reassurance, or urgent response to a sudden health event, dedicated caregivers are always present in the home.

Our facility is equipped with modern safety systems, secure egress monitoring, and clear emergency response protocols coordinated with Ashwaubenon first responders and nearby medical emergency departments.

A Calm, Protective Home Environment

Continuous supervision at HavenSpring is designed to feel protective yet non-intrusive. We foster a peaceful, home-like atmosphere where residents feel secure and relaxed, knowing that compassionate help is just a moment away at any hour of the day or night.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are staff members awake throughout the overnight hours?

Yes, HavenSpring provides active, awake overnight supervision to assist with nighttime mobility, restroom visits, and emergency monitoring.

What happens in the event of a medical emergency?

Our staff are trained in first aid and CPR, immediately initiate emergency protocols with local 911 dispatch, and promptly notify family members and designated healthcare representatives.
